Clement Onyekachi Ugwu, preferably called Jamesclement, is an early-career interdisciplinary researcher based in Abuja, Nigeria. His work spans Health, Agriculture and Finance (HAF), supported by methodological expertise in applied econometrics (including panel-data, time-series and survival analysis), evidence synthesis (systematic reviews and meta-analysis), and statistical and predictive analytics, using Stata, R, Python and SQL. In health, the focus is epidemiology and population health, with particular interest in trachoma, malaria and other vector-borne neglected tropical diseases (NTDs). His work in this area earned him his first grant, awarded by the Royal Society of Tropical Medicine and Hygiene (RSTMH) and funded by the Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF), both based in London.

Agriculture is the second strand. Jamesclement studies how climate-related shocks affect agricultural productivity, and how precision agriculture and sustainable food production can help eradicate poverty. The conviction behind this work is simple: "Nobody deserves to be poor and underfed."

Research, in his view, must solve a problem. It should go beyond publication to strengthen institutions, improve lives and inform policy, or lead to a product or market innovation. Guiding all of it is RETIRE, a framework he developed for research that is Rigorous, Evidence-driven, Transparent, Impact-oriented, Reproducible and Ethically responsible.

Within the Policy Analysis and Research Group (PARG) at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, Jamesclement was elected Director of Research and Academic Planning (DRAP) and, in 2021, received two awards: Best Researcher and Most Resourceful Member of PARG. His professional memberships include RSTMH, Data Science Nigeria (DSN), the Nigerian Statistical Association (NSA) and the Nigerian Economic Society (NES), alongside a digital membership of CFA Institute (not a charterholder or candidate).

Jamesclement is also a co-author and methodology co-designer of a 2025 collaborative research proposal on liposomal harmine-induced apoptosis in triple-negative breast cancer, using Western blot and RT-PCR analysis, which was subsequently funded. Beyond research, he leads the Independent Centre for Experiments, Research, and Programmes (ICERP), a small not-for-profit organisation.

On the economics and finance side, he is currently developing Refine 7, a research startup building proprietary global data platforms on markets, economies and consumer opinion, to give people and businesses better research and advice. His quantitative finance skills include discounted cash flow valuation, risk analysis, market sizing and financial modelling.

Collaboration runs through the whole career. Jamesclement has worked with researchers and scholars of different ranks across academic institutions, and has contributed to more than 100 academic and market research projects across Africa, Europe, North America and Asia. Able to hit the ground running with minimal guidance, he brings a researcher's rigour, a teacher's clarity and a communicator's creativity to every project. He is open to unlearning, relearning and learning new things, and, more importantly, he is committed to being a resource to himself, his team, his supervisors and his institutions.
